Operating system for reusable packaging

The new standard
in reuse.

Asset-level accountability. Enforceable circulation rules.
Built-in resilience — across every partner, location and flow.

Scroll to follow the loop

  1. Each crate, tray and pallet gets a digital identity. QR, barcode or RFID — Rotion adopts the codes you already use, aligned with GS1, so assets move freely across partners and systems.

  2. Every scan records who handled an asset, where, in which role and for how long. Warehouses, transport and retail backrooms all contribute to a single operational truth — from day one.

  3. Circulation rules enforce return windows, deposits clear automatically between partners, and leaks surface before they become losses.

  4. Cycle times, pool health, loss attribution: every rotation makes the system smarter — so you scale reuse with confidence, not guesswork.
